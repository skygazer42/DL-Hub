## Status Contract

- `covered`: already implemented on the target surface
- `pending`: known gap, not yet assigned to a batch
- `planned`: assigned to an active batch manifest
- `merged`: batch landed on `main`
- `out_of_scope`: intentionally excluded from executable batches
